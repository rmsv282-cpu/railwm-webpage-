railwm0.1.0

A scrollable window manager for X11.

railwm is made with AI help and is still early (0.1.0).

A demo of the idea. Open a window and watch: the others keep their size and the view scrolls to the new one.

How it works

Windows sit side by side on a single strip that runs past the edge of your display. Your screen is a viewport onto that strip.

Opening a window adds it to the strip instead of squeezing everything else smaller. Move focus and the view slides along the rail to follow you.
